# FAQ — Frequently Asked IT Questions

**nexthop Employee Portal**  
Version 1.0

## How can I ask the IT team for help?

In the Employee Portal, open **Support** and select **Open support request**.

Explain the problem and provide as much information as possible.

## I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the login page, select **Forgot password** and follow the displayed process.

The request will be sent for approval by the IT team.

## My computer does not turn on. What should I do?

First check:

- power supply;
- charger;
- cables;
- power outlet;
- device indicator lights.

If the problem continues, open a support request.

## My computer is very slow. What should I do?

Include the following in your request:

- when the problem started;
- whether it always happens or only with certain applications;
- which applications are affected;
- any error messages.

Avoid installing programs to try to solve the problem without IT authorization.

## Can I install programs?

Do not install software without authorization when the internal policy requires approval.

If you need an application for work, contact IT.

## I lost my company computer or phone. What should I do?

Contact IT immediately and provide:

- the lost equipment;
- approximate location;
- when the loss was detected;
- any other relevant information.

## I received a suspicious email. What should I do?

Do not click links, open attachments or provide credentials.

Contact the IT team through the Employee Portal.

## Can I use a personal USB drive?

The use of external devices must follow the company's security rules. If in doubt, check with IT.

## How can I see the equipment assigned to me?

In the Employee Portal, open **My equipment**.

There you can view the equipment currently assigned to your profile.

## How do I make a reservation?

Open **Reservations**, select **New reservation**, complete the details and submit the request.

The reservation will wait for IT team approval.

## Can I cancel a reservation?

Reservations that are still pending can be cancelled through the Employee Portal.

## How can I see my notifications?

Click the bell icon in the top-right corner of the Employee Portal.

## My profile information is incorrect. What should I do?

Contact the appropriate company team or the IT team, according to the company's internal procedure.

## Who should I contact in case of a security emergency?

Immediately use the channel defined by the company for security incidents. In the Employee Portal, you can also open a support request for the IT team.
