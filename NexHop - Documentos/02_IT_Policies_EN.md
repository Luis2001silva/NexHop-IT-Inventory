# IT Policies

**NexHop Employee Portal**  
Version 1.0

> This document presents general rules for the use of the company's technology resources. It should be adapted by the company to its internal policies, legal requirements and official procedures.

## 1. Equipment Use

Computers, mobile phones, monitors and other equipment provided by the company are primarily intended for professional activities.

Employees must:

- use equipment responsibly;
- protect equipment from damage;
- report technical problems;
- not remove inventory labels;
- not install unauthorized software.

## 2. Credentials and Passwords

Credentials are personal and must not be shared.

It is prohibited to:

- share passwords;
- use another employee's credentials;
- attempt to access accounts without authorization;
- store credentials insecurely.

Any suspected account compromise must be reported to IT.

## 3. Software

Software installation must follow the rules defined by the company.

The following must not be installed:

- pirated software;
- software from untrusted sources;
- applications without a professional need;
- suspicious browser extensions.

## 4. Security

All employees must contribute to the security of the company's systems.

Good practices:

- lock the computer when it is not being used;
- keep the system up to date;
- do not open suspicious attachments;
- verify unusual requests for credentials;
- use only authorized services.

## 5. Company Data

Company information must be treated as confidential and stored in locations authorized by the company.

Internal data must not be sent to personal services or unauthorized platforms.

## 6. Equipment Outside Company Premises

When company equipment is used outside the company's premises:

- it must remain under the employee's control;
- it must not be left exposed in vehicles;
- secure networks should be used whenever possible;
- any loss or theft must be reported immediately.

## 7. USB and External Devices

The use of USB devices must follow the company's internal security rules.

Unknown devices must not be connected to company computers.

## 8. Security Incidents

The following must be reported immediately:

- lost or stolen equipment;
- suspected phishing;
- malware;
- unauthorized access;
- accidental exposure of information;
- misuse of an account.

## 9. IT Support

Requests should be submitted through the Employee Portal whenever that channel is available.

Employees should provide enough information to allow the issue to be investigated.

## 10. Responsibility

Failure to comply with internal policies may be handled according to the applicable company procedures and regulations.

**Note:** The company's official policies always take precedence over this document.
