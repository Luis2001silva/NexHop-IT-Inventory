# IT Guides and Manuals

**nexthop Employee Portal**  
Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document provides quick guidance for employees on the correct use of the IT equipment and services provided by the company.

## 2. Computer / Laptop

### Sign in

1. Turn on the device.
2. Use the professional credentials provided by the company.
3. Never share your password with anyone.
4. Lock the computer when you leave your desk.

**Windows lock shortcut:** `Windows + L`

### Startup problems

If the computer does not start:

- check that the charger is connected;
- check the power outlet;
- try turning it on again;
- if the problem continues, open a support request in the Employee Portal.

## 3. Password

- Never share your password.
- Do not use the same password for personal and professional services.
- Do not store passwords in text files or on post-it notes.
- If you suspect that your password has been compromised, contact IT immediately.

## 4. Professional Email

Pay attention to:

- unknown senders;
- unexpected links;
- suspicious attachments;
- urgent requests for payment or credentials.

If you receive a suspicious message, do not click links or open attachments. Contact IT.

## 5. Assigned Equipment

Equipment assigned to employees must:

- be used according to internal rules;
- be kept in good condition;
- not be lent to third parties without authorization;
- be reported to IT in case of loss, theft or damage.

## 6. VPN and Remote Access

When the company provides remote access or VPN:

1. Use only approved tools.
2. Avoid public Wi-Fi networks for sensitive tasks.
3. Sign out when you finish working.

## 7. Support Request

For IT problems:

1. Go to **Employee Portal → Support**.
2. Choose a clear subject.
3. Explain the problem.
4. Include error messages, if any.
5. Add relevant information about the equipment.

The more information you provide, the easier it will be for the IT team to diagnose the problem.

## 8. Loss or Theft

In case of lost or stolen equipment:

1. Contact IT immediately.
2. Inform the appropriate internal manager, when applicable.
3. Identify the affected equipment.
4. Do not attempt to recover the equipment if doing so puts you at risk.

## 9. Contact IT

For any technical problem, use **Employee Portal → Support**.
